01 · The scientific question

One molecule, two questions.

ABBV-CLS-7262

Fosigotifator

eIF2B activator; prodrug of 2BAct, ISRIB lineage (UCSF, 2013). Oral, once daily.

VWM · a genetic disease of eIF2B

Vanishing white matter disease

Biallelic EIF2B variants, early-childhood onset, no approved therapy. Open-label Phase 1b/2: adults first, then adolescents, children, infants; MRI and biomarkers.

… and a longer list of hypotheses, kept unnamed here

ALS · a mechanistic hypothesis

Amyotrophic lateral sclerosis

Hypothesis: a chronic integrated stress response drives degeneration. AbbVie Phase 1 in 31 patients (drug measured in CSF), then HEALEY Regimen F: 310 patients, shared placebo, 24 weeks.

Phase 1 in 158 healthy volunteers (AAN 2023): well tolerated to 14 days; eIF2B activity up and the stress response down in blood cells. From there, each question needed its own evidence plan, sponsor of record and regulatory path.

04 · The pushback

Two points of pushback.

With the partner

I asked that the VWM path open in parallel with ALS, not after its readout.

The paper, written with Competitive Intelligence and Business Development, carried the argument: the ALS Phase 1 was already giving us safety, PK and CSF exposure in patients; the mouse rescue was the strongest preclinical evidence we had; an orphan route and a paediatric plan made the regulatory path tractable; and a 50-patient, five-centre study cost a fraction of the 310-patient regimen.

Agreed outcome: advance both clinical paths. Both studies opened in March 2023, ten days apart.

Within our evaluation group

The best-argued dossier could outweigh the evidence.

I co-led the process and owned my part of the problem. I raised it with the PTL and the group, and we moved to a common evidence review and scoring approach: the same criteria for every case, the assumptions written down, the expert review recorded.

Both pushbacks had the same shape: put the evidence where everyone could inspect it.

06 · The contract layer

Applying the transition provisions.

The public option: AbbVie could take on late-stage development and commercialization after Phase 2a. The program reality: the VWM study ran with Calico as sponsor of record and AbbVie funding; the ALS Phase 1 under AbbVie; the Phase 2/3 regimen under a sponsor-investigator at MGH. The question: which event triggered which responsibility, for which study?

My work

Worked through how the provisions applied to each study with AbbVie. In 2024 the EU paediatric plan moved to AbbVie and expanded access opened under AbbVie sponsorship while the study stayed under Calico; I worked on both. Opt-in and opt-out transition plans: sponsorship, contracts, data and budget, through close-out.

The question

Which event triggers which responsibility, for which study?

Opt-in scenario

  • Transition plan: regulatory sponsorship, INDs and CTAs, data package
  • Contracts, vendors and sites: transition services
  • Joint plan and budget; cost share continues

Opt-out scenario

  • Continuation under one company; rights and data returned
  • Partner-sponsored studies: transfer or close-out
  • Obligations, vendors and budget through wind-down

After my time · Nov 2025: the collaboration ended; Calico carried the molecule forward alone. In outline, the opt-out branch.

09 · What I would change

Four things I would do earlier.

01

Define the evidence the next decision needs, earlier

Before the work is funded: what result would change the plan, and what would not.

02

Review early deliverables before the work is complete

A first data package or interim read, checked against the specification while there is still time to correct it.

03

Clarify transition events before the plan is final

With the scientific, regulatory, alliance and legal owners in the room: sponsor of record, option event and transition services named for each study, so no one reads a phase label as a decision.

04

Bring the comparative analysis and scenario plans forward

Bring Competitive Intelligence and Business Development into the comparison before positions harden.
